## Adjudication-derived policy changes

### 0144 - target construct rule

The paper uses systematically modified BESSI-derived items, but the resulting variables measure change goals and feasibility beliefs rather than participants' SEB skill levels/capacities. It is therefore review-eligible but quantitatively ineligible.

This produced the stable rule that **target construct is separate from item provenance**. The new exclusion reason is `NO_ELIGIBLE_SEB_SKILL_CONSTRUCT`.

### 0145 - component-specific quantitative eligibility

The publication contains multiple studies, but only Study 2 uses eligible BESSI measurement and contributes to the quantitative database. Study 1 is not coded as a quantitative sample.

This produced the stable rule that publication-level meta eligibility may be satisfied by one or more internal components, recorded in `eligible_quantitative_components`. Only eligible components later receive sample/matrix IDs.

### 0148 - framework-relation tagging

The paper is retained for the review because it substantively integrates and extends/modifies the BESSI/SEB framework within a proposed alternative framework. This relationship must remain explicit when the richer review topic/keyword taxonomy is developed.

This produced `framework_relation_tags`, including `EXTENDS_OR_MODIFIES_SEB_FRAMEWORK` and `COMPARES_SEB_WITH_OTHER_FRAMEWORKS`.
